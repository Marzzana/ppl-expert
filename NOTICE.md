## About the Hebrew layer

The Hebrew translation, the code walkthroughs, the exercises and the glossary are
original work by this repository's author, written with Claude Code. They are
offered as a study aid and are **not official course material**: they are not
written, reviewed or endorsed by the course staff. Where a model answer is given
for a practice question that the course did not publish a solution for, it is
labelled as such in the app. When the two disagree, the course is right.
